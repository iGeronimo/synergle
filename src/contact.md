Helpful things to include:

- **Puzzle feedback:** the puzzle number (shown above the board) and which group felt wrong.
- **Bug reports:** your device and browser, and what happened. A screenshot helps.
- **Category ideas:** connections between champions that you'd like to see in a future puzzle.

We read everything, though we can't always reply individually.
