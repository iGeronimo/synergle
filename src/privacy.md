This policy explains how **{{ site.name }}** ("we", "us") at {{ site.url }} handles information. Questions? Email [{{ site.contactEmail }}](mailto:{{ site.contactEmail }}).

## Information stored on your device

Your game progress, statistics, streak and settings are saved in your browser's **local storage** so you can pick up where you left off. This data stays on your device. We never receive it, and you can delete it at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

We don't require accounts and we don't ask for your name, email address or any other personal details to play.

<h2 id="advertising">Advertising</h2>

{{ site.name }} is funded by ads served by **Google AdSense**.

- Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website and other websites.
- Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
- You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/). You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at [www.aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/).
- To learn more, read [how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

Ad partners may collect information such as your IP address, device and browser type, and the pages you view, and may use cookies, web beacons or similar technologies. Their use of that information is governed by their own privacy policies.

<h2 id="cookies">Cookies and consent</h2>

If you visit from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you'll be asked for consent before personalized ads or non-essential cookies are used. That consent message is provided by Google, a certified consent management platform. You can change your choices at any time with the **Privacy settings** link in the footer. If you decline, you may still see non-personalized ads.

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The game keeps working without them, although saved progress needs local storage.

## Analytics

We may use Google Analytics to understand how many people play and which pages are popular. It collects aggregated usage information such as pages viewed, approximate location (country or city), device type and referring site. Where consent is required, analytics only runs after you agree. You can install the [Google Analytics opt-out add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout) to opt out entirely.

## Server logs

Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically log technical information such as IP addresses, browser type and request times. This helps keep the site secure and working. These logs are kept for a limited time.

## Children

{{ site.name }} is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, including the EEA, the UK and some US states such as California, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising. Because we don't store personal data ourselves, most requests are best handled through the consent tools above, [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/), or by contacting Google directly. You can always contact us at [{{ site.contactEmail }}](mailto:{{ site.contactEmail }}).

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example if we add new features or ad partners. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
